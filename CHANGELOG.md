# Sötét Képernyő: changelog

## v1.4 (8 November 2026): voices

### Voice acting
- The narrator and the characters now speak: about 600 lines, an hour of voice across all nine episodes and Lépcsőház (the intro, the walks, the calls, the found phone's inbox, the messages at home).
- Every character has their own voice: Nagyi, Mum, Ms Varga, VELA, Bence, Dóri, Lili, Laci, Feri bácsi and the rest. Phone calls sound like phone calls, VELA sounds like a speaker, chat messages are read in the sender's voice.
- Scammers who pretend to be someone you know speak in that person's voice, like a cloned voice would.
- The music dips while someone speaks, captions stay up until the line is finished, and the Lépcsőház intro waits for the narrator.
- Settings: Voice acting on/off and Voice volume. Off brings back the old mumbling sounds.
- Lines that contain your nickname or a number still use the mumbling sounds for now.

### Phones and tablets (the app and phone browsers)
- The touch layer from the app build is now part of the game: joysticks, a Use button, tilt to look (gyroscope), an on-screen keyboard with suggestions, and vibration that follows the sounds.
- A setup in up to four steps at the first start: brightness, screen orientation (phones), text size and subtitles, voices and the gyroscope. "Setup again" in Settings.
- Tap a dotted word to read its explanation. Tap to skip the text screens in Lépcsőház. Swipe up for the next clip in the feed episode. Parts follow your finger in the computer build.
- Phone replies at home can be sent with a button on touch screens; "Not now" closes them without Esc.
- The pause menu, the teacher's computer and the Lépcsőház results show how many stories you have finished.
- A new scene never starts paused or behind an old menu, and the "E: use" prompt hides while a menu is open.

### Fixes
- Episode 1: you can look through the peephole a little longer, while Mrs Kovács is still on the landing, and the narrator starts with the picture. On touch screens she takes longer to find you.
- The title screen said v1.2.
- The phone layer is also in the school page (game/index.html), so the GitHub version works on phones. It only switches on with a touch screen.

## v1.3 (Lépcsőház)

### Lépcsőház (Stairwell), 13+
- A new door in Room 214 opens a second, replayable game: a flat in a Budapest panel block and a night out every time, with a topic, a route and the places on it.
- The stairwell (spot what is different on a landing), eight walk places where figures say what manipulators say and you type the safe reply, the school, the last metro, and a message waiting on the phone at home.
- Twelve topics, five routes, 214 notebook cards, a flat to furnish with paintings, stickers and posters, the endless Labyrinth, and the Tenant as a hard mode.
- Camera effects: motion blur, depth of field, chromatic aberration, vignette and film grain, with sliders for depth of field and motion blur.
- The Teacher Board reads Lépcsőház saves: nights, cards, best scores and the last nights per student.
- The public build has a bus made in code at the bus station.

## v1.2 (13 October 2026)

### Episode 2: Mindig figyel (Always listening)
- Every device you haven't secured now comes alive in the dark, at most twice each: the TV shows your own room as "Recommended for you", the robot vacuum maps a person standing in the hallway, the doorbell chime plays back your voice, VELA quotes what you said, and the PC camera light blinks while it greets you by name. Devices you haven't found yet go first, so they lead you to them. Securing a device stops it for good.
- At midnight VELA runs its nightly cloud backup and lists everything the unsecured devices recorded, with your own voice played back over itself. Devices you never found show up as "Unknown device". Secure all six and the backup is empty.
- Finding the teddy bear no longer ends the night: you can only call it done once all six devices have been found, or from 23:56.
- The end screen shows how many devices were still listening at midnight.

### Episode 1: Késő esti szerelés (Late-night build)
- If you're stuck on a part for a while, you remember which room you saw it in; a little later it glints from across the room, even with the flashlight off.
- The graphics card can't get lost any more: the wardrobe always gives it back, and you get a nudge towards the wardrobe if it stays missing.
- A part you've just fitted no longer follows the mouse around the fitting screen.

### Everything else
- The windows in the flat, Nagyi's flat and the school lab show painted views of the panel blocks outside, and the view shifts as you walk past.
- The meadow after the finale has proper trees, flowers, grass and rocks, a painted sunset sky with drifting clouds, birdsong and new music. The ground and plants no longer shimmer as you walk.
- "Still Alive" plays right on the page when the game runs on a website.
- The title screen says nine stories, not six.
- You can no longer walk backwards out of the lab through the closed door.
- Classroom version: lesson codes for Episodes 2 and 3 changed so they don't hint at the answers (the old ones still work), the end screen's "next episode" button asks for the code like the lab computers do, and the codes are no longer readable in the game's source.
