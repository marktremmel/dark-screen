Sötét Képernyő (Dark Screen): privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. Sötét Képernyő has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any information about you or your device to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress (your nickname, finished episodes, your flat and notebook in Lépcsőház, and your settings) is saved only on your own device. If you choose to copy a save code or save a save file, that is something you do yourself; the app never sends it anywhere.

In Lépcsőház, the answer you write "in your own words" at the end of a night is stored only in your save on this device and in any save code you copy. Your teacher can only read it if you give them your save code.

Internet

The game runs offline. The only link to the internet is an optional easter egg after the finale, which opens a YouTube video in your browser if you choose to watch it. YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
