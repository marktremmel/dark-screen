SÖTÉT KÉPERNYŐ (Dark Screen) v1.3 - a digital culture horror anthology
====================================================================

HOW TO START
Double-click index.html. It opens in Chrome or Edge and runs offline:
no internet, no installation, no web server needed. Keep the "data"
folder next to index.html.

If a school computer refuses to open local files, copy the whole folder
to the desktop first, or put it on any web space (school server, etc.).

CONTROLS
Mouse: look around (click the picture first)   W A S D / arrows: walk
Shift: run (makes noise!)   E: use / pick up   F: flashlight
Q: put a carried part back   R: rotate the part in the fitting screen   Esc: pause

WHAT IS IN IT NOW
- The hub: an empty school computer lab at night. Each old PC is one
  episode. A finished episode leaves its PC glowing and unlocks the next.
Play order (the lab has nine computers):
  1 Késő esti szerelés (3D) · 2 Mindig figyel (3D) · 3 Valaki tudja (phone)
  4 Kamerák (3D) · 5 A csoportchat (laptop) · 6 Zárolva (3D)
  7 Végtelen hírfolyam (phone) · 8 Anya hangja (3D) · 9 Tükörkép (laptop)
- Episode 1 "Késő esti szerelés" (Late-night build), about 12-15 minutes.
- Episode 2 "Mindig figyel" (Always listening), about 12-14 minutes.
  Tab opens the VELA Home app on the in-game tablet.
- Episode 3 "Valaki tudja" (Someone knows), about 12-14 minutes.
  Played on a phone with the mouse only. The notebook on the right
  shows the countdown, what the stranger knows, a Hint and a Pause button.
- Episode 4 "Kamerák" (Cameras), about 12-15 minutes. A shopping centre
  after closing. H pulls the hood up. Stay out of the red camera fans
  and the night guard's torch.
- Episode 5 "A csoportchat" (The group chat), about 10-13 minutes.
  Played on a laptop desktop with the mouse. Hover over a message for
  the 📸 screenshot button.
- Episode 6 "Zárolva" (Locked), about 12-15 minutes. A school sleepover
  at night when ransomware locks every computer. Ten minutes on the
  clock to save the backup disk. The checklist top left shows the goals.
- Episode 8 "Anya hangja" (Mum's voice), about 12-15 minutes. Nagyi's flat,
  a phone call in Mum's voice, and a "lawyer" coming at midnight. The rotary
  phone in the hall really dials: click the numbers.
- Episode 7 "Végtelen hírfolyam" (Endless feed), about 12 minutes. Played
  on a phone with the mouse. Press "Spot a trick" before tapping a trick.
- Episode 9 "Tükörkép" (Reflection), about 15 minutes, the finale. Played
  on a laptop desktop. After it, the lab menu switches to morning, and the
  lab door leads outside to a meadow at sunset (an easter egg). The old
  computer in the meadow plays "Still Alive" (YouTube, needs internet:
  in the page when the game is played from the web, in a new tab when
  index.html is opened from a folder).
- Characters "speak" with made-up voice sounds; the subtitles carry the words.

LÉPCSŐHÁZ (Stairwell), 13+, new in v1.3
The door next to the computers in Room 214 opens a second, replayable
game. The first time it asks for a code (in the Teacher Pack; the
teacher key also opens it). Every night out has a topic, a route and a
few places: the stairwell (turn back if a landing is different), a
walk where you type safe replies, the school, the last metro, and a
message waiting on the phone at home. Answers become notebook cards,
coins furnish the flat. The Labyrinth is an endless high-score mode.
The Lépcsőház guide is in the Teacher Pack.
Settings for it: Calm difficulty, Hard typing, the Tenant (hard mode),
camera effects with sliders for depth of field and motion blur
(switch them off on slow computers).

LOCKED EPISODES (lesson codes)
Episode 1 is always open. Every later episode is locked until the
student types that lesson's code, which the teacher writes on the board
(the codes are in the Teacher Pack, not in this folder). Episodes also
still open in order: an episode needs the one before it finished.
Teacher's computer (front of the lab) -> "Teacher key" opens every
episode on one nickname, for previewing.

SAVE CODES AND THE TEACHER BOARD
The end screen of every episode shows the student's save code with a
"Copy the code" button. Students paste it into their reflection sheet.
The teacher pastes the codes into Teacher_Board.html (Teacher Pack) to
see who finished which episode, with times, stats and choices.

SAVING
Progress saves automatically per nickname on this computer (browser
storage). To move to another computer: Pause (Esc) or the teacher's PC
at the front of the lab -> "Save code" -> copy the SK1-... code,
then "Load a save code" on the title screen of the other computer.
Mid-episode progress is kept: fitted parts stay fitted.

SETTINGS (title screen, pause menu, or the teacher's PC)
Quiet mode (csendes mód): removes the jump scares, story unchanged.
Brightness + Projector mode: use these if the picture is too dark on
the projector. Sound subtitles, large text, head bobbing, mouse speed.

RESETTING
Each student uses their own nickname. To wipe all saves on a computer,
clear the browser's site data for this file.
